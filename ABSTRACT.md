# PROJECT ABSTRACT

**Project Title:** EduTouch – Smart Braille E-Learning Platform

**Abstract:**
This project focuses on providing an accessible e-learning platform for blind and visually impaired students. It supports bilingual learning in both English and Tamil. Key features include converting text and documents (PDF, Word, PPT) into Braille notation, a voice assistant for hands-free navigation, and automated quiz generation. Students can download lessons in Braille PDF or BRF formats and listen to audio versions of their lessons. The system is designed to help students learn independently with minimal assistance.

---

## PROJECT DISADVANTAGES & LIMITATIONS

*   **Tactile Hardware Requirement:** While the platform generates Braille files (PDF/BRF), students still require a specialized Braille printer (embosser) or a refreshable Braille display to physically feel the tactile dots.
*   **Grade 1 Braille Focus:** The current implementation primarily supports Grade 1 Braille (character-by-character translation). Advanced Grade 2 (contracted) Braille notation is not yet fully integrated.
*   **Connectivity Dependency:** Some advanced accessibility features, such as high-quality neural text-to-speech, may require an active internet connection to interact with cloud engines.
*   **Acoustic Sensitivity:** The voice-controlled assistant performs best in quiet environments; high levels of ambient classroom noise can occasionally interfere with command accuracy.
*   **Visual Validation:** Instructors who do not know Braille must rely on the platform's visual "original text" preview to verify the content being taught to students.
